# A Century of Slides

A slide-projector slideshow of 136 photographs from the first hundred years of
Myers Park Presbyterian Church in Charlotte, North Carolina (1926–2026).

**Open it:** https://joshutk.github.io/mppc-century-of-slides/

Pick one of six slide trays, then press **Forward**. Each change runs like an
old carousel projector. The slide drops out, the lamp shines through the
empty gate, the tray turns, and the next slide comes up into focus, all in
time with a recording of a real projector.

- **Trays:** Buildings & Grounds · Worship & Music · Congregational Life · Children & Youth · Mission & Outreach · Pastors & Leaders
- **Controls:**
  - Forward and Reverse buttons, or swipe across the picture
  - The arrow keys, Space and Page Up/Down also work
  - Esc returns to the trays, Home goes back to the first slide, and M mutes
- **Sound:** on by default, with a mute button. The setting is remembered.
- **Reduced motion:** `?lite=1` gives a plain crossfade, as does the system's reduced-motion setting.

## Running it as a kiosk
It is a static page with no server code and works in any current browser. It also runs from a local copy: open `index.html`.
- **iPad:** open the address above in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen, then turn on Guided Access.
- **Touch TV or PC:** run Chrome in kiosk mode (`--kiosk`) pointed at the address.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole app, with styles, scripts, fonts and sound built in
- `slides/<tray>/`: the photographs, 1600 px on the long edge
- `src/`: the sources `index.html` is built from. `data.js` (slides and captions) and `audio.js` (the sound) are generated by the curation pipeline, which is kept privately with the original photo archive.
- `build.py`: rebuilds `index.html` from `src/` (`python build.py`)
